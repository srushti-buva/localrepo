About Us

The story behind the sweets

Our story

Jaswant Sweets Ujaliwadi started as a small family shop serving the people of Ujaliwadi and nearby villages. What began with a few trays of laddu and jalebi has grown into a name locals trust for every celebration.

We still prepare our sweets in small batches using the recipes we started with. Customers come to us for birthdays, festivals and weddings, and many have been coming for years.
